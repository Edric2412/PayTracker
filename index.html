A few useful answers

Before you get started.

How does transaction detection work?

Pay Tracker scans supported payment notifications (like Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) and local bank SMS debit messages in real time using private on-device heuristics.

Do I need to review transactions?

You can review new expenses in the Review Queue with a single tap, confirm suggestions, or edit categories.

Why does the app request notification or SMS access?

Notification listener detects transactions as they happen. SMS access is strictly optional to import historical bank transaction alerts.

Is my financial data uploaded anywhere?

Never. Pay Tracker does not have any external servers. All data stays strictly in your private local SQLite database on your device.
