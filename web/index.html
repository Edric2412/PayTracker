📱 How to Install on Android

Since Pay Tracker is 100% independent and open-source, here is how to install the APK directly:

Step 1

Download the APK

Tap the green download button above. If sent via WhatsApp, tap the 3 dots and choose "Save to Downloads".

Step 2

Open via Files App

Open your phone's built-in Files by Google (or My Files), open Downloads, and tap PayTracker-v1.0.apk.

Step 3

Install Anyway

If Google Play Protect asks, tap "More details" ➡️ "Install anyway". That's it!

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about privacy, permissions, and security.

Why isn't Pay Tracker on the Google Play Store?

Google charges a $25 developer registration fee and enforces complex multi-week closed testing requirements. Distributing directly via our website keeps Pay Tracker 100% free and independent.

Does Pay Tracker read my personal SMS or OTPs?

Never. The local parser strictly scans for bank debit/credit keywords (e.g., INR, debited, credited, VPA). Personal chats, messages, and OTPs are completely ignored and discarded.

Is my financial data uploaded anywhere?

No. Pay Tracker does not even connect to an external server. You can turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data, and the app functions 100% normally.

How do I update Pay Tracker?

You can check back on this website anytime or use Obtainium to get automatic update notifications directly from our GitHub releases!
