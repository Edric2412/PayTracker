Is my data safe with Pay Tracker? ▼
Yes, 100%. Pay Tracker has no external servers or analytics SDKs. All transactions remain strictly within your device's private SQLite database.
Does it work offline? ▼
Completely. You can turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data, and Pay Tracker will continue to parse alerts, categorize expenses, and track your cash flow seamlessly.
Why is it asking for permissions? ▼
Notification Listener is required to detect Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm alerts in real time. SMS access is optional to scan bank debit history.
